02 / THE METHOD

Patterns, with
perspective.

01

Bring the context

Twelve BRFSS survey inputs describe sleep, physical health, social support and personal context.

02

Compare every model

Three classical models and three neural networks each predict a stress category. The ensemble uses majority voting.

03

Understand the limits

Trained on 30,000 unique CDC BRFSS 2022 respondent records. Predicts reported stress frequency, not a diagnosis or future stress. US survey findings may not generalize elsewhere.

03 / THE EVIDENCE

Behind the models.

BRFSS HOLDOUT · 5,998 RESPONDENTS

Held-out respondents; identical input profiles stay in one split. Unweighted complete-case sample, not a population estimate. Majority-class baseline accuracy: 64.85%; macro F1: 0.2623. Class-weighted models prioritize all three classes, not accuracy alone.
